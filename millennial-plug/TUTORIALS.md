# Tutorials — the short path, not the long one

Every link below was checked to confirm it resolves and that the title and channel are what I say they are.

**Read this first:** you do not need a web design education to fix your site. Most of what makes Morning Brew look "official" is four decisions — color, type, spacing, hierarchy — made once and then applied consistently. The Day 1 list below is 41 minutes and covers roughly 80% of the gap. Everything after that is optional depth.

Skip anything about Figma, Webflow, or HTML/CSS unless you find you enjoy it. You're working inside Beehiiv's builder, so those are detours.

## Steal from these directly

Faster than any tutorial: open these, screenshot the sections that work, and note *why*.

- **[Big Desk Energy](https://mail.bigdeskenergy.com/)** — your closest match by a wide margin. Explicitly Windows 98–inspired, and it's built on Beehiiv, so everything you see there is achievable on your plan. Its designer published a [section-by-section breakdown of how it was built](https://readpixels.beehiiv.com/p/big-desk-energy-breakdown-tyler-denk/) — animated 3D logo, custom Canva section headers, retro buttons, layered footer. That article is the single most useful thing on this page for you.
- **[Milk Road](https://milkroad.com/)** — the milk-carton perforated dividers and heavy branded border. Proof that one repeated visual motif can carry an entire identity.
- **[Morning Brew](https://www.morningbrew.com/)** — restraint and hierarchy. Study the spacing and how the article grid is organized, not the colors.
- **[The Hustle](https://thehustle.co/)** — voice and personality inside a clean, corporate-safe layout. Closest to the tone you're going for.

**One caution on Big Desk Energy:** it gets away with heavy retro styling because the underlying layout discipline is strict — consistent spacing, one accent per section, clean body type. Copy the discipline before the decoration. Retro without the discipline is just cluttered.

## What to actually do with this

1. Watch Day 1 (41 min).
2. Open `preview.html` and change values in the `:root` block at the top. Break it, undo it. Fastest way to build intuition.
3. Watch the Day 3 Beehiiv videos with your builder open in a second tab.
4. Rebuild your homepage in the order listed in `BRAND-SYSTEM.md` §4, using native Beehiiv blocks for anything that captures email and the files in `beehiiv-blocks/` for the retro chrome.
5. Build the **Advertise** page. With 1.5M followers, that's the page that turns this into revenue.
